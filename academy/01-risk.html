01 · Risk first

A setup without a stop is a hope. Moe AI always publishes a stop at the origin of the breakout leg. If that level breaks, the idea is wrong.

Rules to keep

  • Decide risk in R before size in coins.
  • One idea per pair. Do not stack five versions of the same trade.
  • Missed entries are cheaper than chasing.
  • A win rate without R-multiple is marketing.

Homework: write your max loss per idea before you open Discord.
