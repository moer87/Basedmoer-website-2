02 · Market structure

Trend is a sequence, not a feeling. In a bullish structure the market prints higher highs and higher lows. In a bearish structure it prints lower highs and lower lows.

Read it on 4H

Moe AI uses a 50-candle window and looks for the latest local high/low that was actually broken. Ranges exist. If there is no clean sequence, there is no trade.

Homework: mark HH/HL or LH/LL on one pair you already watch. Do not forecast. Only label what already printed.
