03 · Break of structure

A break of structure is one candle that takes the prior swing high (bullish) or swing low (bearish). Body size does not have to be huge. The close and the following pullback matter more than a dramatic wick.

False breaks happen. That is why the scanner waits for price to still sit inside the 0.50–0.786 zone when the signal is posted. If price already ran to target, the setup is gone.
