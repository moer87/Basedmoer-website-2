06 · Base and wallets

Base is an Ethereum L2. A wallet is a key, not a login. If a site asks you to sign a contract you do not understand, leave.

  • This site’s Connect button only asks for your address in the browser. It does not mint, spend, or gate NFTs yet.
  • Official mint pages will be announced from @basedmoer and @MoeeR87.
  • Do not enter seed phrases on any Based Moer page. We will never ask.
